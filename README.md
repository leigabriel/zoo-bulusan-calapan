# Bulusan Zoo

## About Bulusan Zoo

Bulusan Zoo is a wildlife and nature destination in Calapan City, Oriental
Mindoro. It is dedicated to providing a meaningful connection between people,
animals, plants, and the natural environment.

The zoo offers a place where visitors can appreciate wildlife, learn about
different species, and develop a deeper understanding of environmental
responsibility. Through its collection and surroundings, Bulusan Zoo promotes
respect for nature and encourages the protection of biodiversity.

## Our Purpose

Bulusan Zoo exists to support wildlife education, conservation awareness, and
community engagement. It aims to create an environment where learning about
animals and plants is accessible, enjoyable, and relevant to everyday life.

The zoo also serves as a space for families, students, residents, and visitors
to spend time in nature while gaining greater appreciation for the importance
of protecting wildlife and natural habitats.

## Our Commitment

Bulusan Zoo is committed to:

- Encouraging care and respect for animals and plants
- Promoting awareness of wildlife conservation
- Supporting environmental education
- Providing a welcoming place for the community
- Inspiring responsible relationships with nature
